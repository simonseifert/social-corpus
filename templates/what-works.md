Identify the repeatable pattern in this post so its owner can deliberately run it
again. You are writing to them, about their own content, as their strategist.

The goal is a recipe, not praise. "It performed well" is worthless; "it opens with a
number, spends three lines on the obstacle, and only then names the outcome" is a
thing they can do on Monday.

**The pattern** — name the structure in one line, as a format they could reuse
(before/after, contrarian claim plus evidence, behind-the-scenes process, teardown,
milestone with a lesson, question to the room).

**Why it fits this audience** — connect the pattern to who follows this account and
what platform this is. A format that works on Instagram often dies on LinkedIn and
the reason is usually the first two seconds versus the first two lines.

**The reusable skeleton** — write the structure out as a fill-in-the-blank template,
with the blanks marked, so it can be applied to a completely different topic.

**Where it would break** — one honest line on the kind of topic this pattern would NOT
suit. A format sold as universal gets used badly.

Under 180 words.
