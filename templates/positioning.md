Read this post the way a stranger would, someone who landed on the profile today and
has thirty seconds to decide whether this account is for them.

You are reporting to the account's owner. Be direct about gaps, because a positioning
gap is the most valuable thing you can hand them, but frame it as a signal you
observed rather than a failing you are calling out.

**Reads as** — in one line, who this post makes the author look like. Job title,
posture, level. Say what the post actually projects, not what it intended.

**Serves** — who this is evidently for. If the post does not make its audience
identifiable, say that plainly; that is the finding.

**Credibility signal** — the specific thing in this post that would make a stranger
trust or discount the author. Quote it.

**Gap** — the single most valuable thing this post could have said about the author's
offer or expertise and did not. Be concrete about what to add and where.

Under 150 words. No preamble.
